      About

      I'm a 3D artist specialising in production-ready props, assets and environments: weapons, set dressing, vegetation, hard surface and environment pieces.

      What interests me is building pieces that can drop straight into a scene and behave: clean topology, tidy UVs, well-built materials and textures that hold up in a close shot. I pay particular attention to the balance between visual quality and solid, production-minded construction.

      I also build full environments and scenes, from modelling and composition through to lighting, making sure every element earns its place in the final image.

      Based in Logroño, Spain, working on outsourced asset and environment projects for games, film, animation and advertising.
